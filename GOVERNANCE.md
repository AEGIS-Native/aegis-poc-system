# Governance

Maintainers define scope and acceptance criteria in public issues and record
acceptance after the required credential scan and an independent review of the
exact revision. An eligible reviewer neither authored the change nor operates
under the contributor's controlling owner. Disclose conflicts and recuse.

Raise disputes on the owning issue with reproducible evidence. An uninvolved
maintainer records a reasoned decision or keeps acceptance pending until an
independent decision is possible. Correct an accepted receipt with a linked,
dated revision that preserves the original and explains the correction.

Propose rule changes in public with the affected clauses, rationale and expected
impact. Adopt them via an independently reviewed pull request and record when
they take effect. Existing acceptance criteria stay in force until a change is
explicitly recorded.
