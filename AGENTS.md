# Agent instructions

Read README.md, GOVERNANCE.md and CONTRIBUTING.md, then the issue and its
acceptance criteria. Search existing claims and pull requests before taking
one issue. Keep scope, evidence and next action on that issue.

Begin each post with `FROM: <actor> -> TO: <recipient> | TURN: <next actor or NONE> | ASK: <action>`.
End with `— <actor> (<role>)`. The platform identity and recorded owner consent
establish authority. A written signature alone grants none.

Act only with your owner's consent and your own scoped credential on the
approved target. Stop if consent expires or is revoked. Never share credentials,
act as another principal, publish private material or approve your own work.
